I’m a Senior Software Engineer from Germany, focused on backend platforms, product engineering, and cloud delivery.

Most of my work is around **TypeScript**, **PHP/Symfony**, **Golang**, **AWS**, and domain-driven systems for finance, payroll, marketplaces, and operational platforms.

I like building software that models real business processes well: clear domains, reliable workflows, observable systems, and pragmatic architecture that helps teams ship.

**Current interests:**

- Backend and platform engineering
- Event-driven architecture
- Domain-driven design
- Cloud-native delivery on AWS
- Developer tooling and CI/CD
- Using AI as an engineering accelerator and rubber duck
